 About Litescan

                
            

            

                

                
Litescan Professional

                
Build v2.0.0

                
Litescan is an offline-capable, privacy-focused client-side application for generating and scanning QR codes, barcodes, and document files with a complete suite of utility tools.

                

                    

                        
                        

                            
100% Client-Side Privacy

                            
No code data or camera feeds are ever sent to external servers.

                        

                    

                    

                        
                        

                            
Instant Audio Feedback

                            
Scan confirmed by a synthesized audio beep using the Web Audio API.

 Privacy Policy

                
            

            

                

                    
1. Introduction

                    
Welcome to Litescan ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our web application.


                    
2. Information We Collect

                    
Litescan is designed with a privacy-first approach. Our application operates almost entirely on the client-side (your device).

                    
    
                        
  • Camera Access: We request access to your device's camera solely for the purpose of scanning QR codes and barcodes. Camera feeds are processed locally on your device and are never transmitted to any external servers.
    • 
                        
  • Local Storage: We use your browser's localStorage to save your scan history, app preferences (such as theme settings), and generated codes. This data remains on your device and is never sent to our servers.
    • 
                        
  • File Uploads: When you use our file scanning or document tools (PDF tools, image tools, OCR), all files are processed entirely within your browser. No files are uploaded to any server.
    • 
                        
  • Personal Information: We do not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number through the app itself.
    • 
                    


                    
3. How We Use Information

                    
The limited data stored locally is used exclusively to:

                    
    
                        
  • Display your scan history for quick reference
    • 
                        
  • Remember your app preferences and theme settings
    • 
                        
  • Improve your user experience within the application
    • 
                    


                    
4. Third-Party Services

                    
Our application may use the following third-party services:

                    
    
                        
  • Google Fonts: We use Google Fonts to provide typography. Google may collect usage data as described in their privacy policy.
    • 
                        
  • CDN Libraries: We load open-source JavaScript libraries from CDNs (such as jsDelivr and unpkg) to power our features. These services may log standard web request data.
    • 
                        
  • Google AdSense: We may display advertisements through Google AdSense. Google uses cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your browsing activity. You can manage your ad preferences through Google's Ad Settings.
    • 
                    


                    
5. Cookies & Tracking

                    
Litescan itself does not set tracking cookies. However, third-party services we integrate (such as Google AdSense) may use cookies. You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings.


                    
6. Data Security

                    
Since all core processing happens on your device, your data benefits from the security of your own device and browser. We do not store or process your data on any external servers, minimizing the risk of data breaches.


                    
7. Children's Privacy

                    
Litescan is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.


                    
8. Changes to This Policy

                    
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted within the application with an updated effective date. Continued use of Litescan after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.


                    
9. Contact Us

                    
If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please reach out to us at:

                    
Email: support@litescan.app
